Technology careers rarely follow a straight line. With every new company, product, or stack, we need to relearn the context, understand constraints, and discover where our work creates the most impact.

Good mentoring helps exactly there. It reduces noise, accelerates feedback, and turns tacit experience into practical conversations. To me, mentoring is not about having every answer, but about asking better questions and creating safety to test paths.

I also believe technical seniority shows up strongly in communication. Explaining trade-offs, documenting decisions, and aligning expectations matter as much as writing code.
